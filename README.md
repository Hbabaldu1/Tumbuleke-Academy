## Overview

Tumbuleke Academy is the official digital learning platform of **Tumbuleke Hub**.

The platform is designed to deliver world-class technology education, professional certification, innovation programs, and digital empowerment opportunities.

It combines a modern Learning Management System (LMS), student management portal, instructor workspace, and administrative dashboard into a single scalable platform.

---

## Vision

To become Africa's leading technology and innovation academy by providing accessible, practical, and industry-relevant digital education.

---

## Mission

To equip learners with future-ready skills through high-quality training, mentorship, and innovation-driven programs.
